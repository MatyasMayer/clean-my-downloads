What counts as the same topic? PROTOTYPE

The sorter puts files into School\<SUBJECT> when the name has a subject code, otherwise into Topics\<stem> when at least two files share a name stem, otherwise they are loose files. This page lets you test how the stem is cut. Change the rules on the left, add or remove filenames, and watch which files end up together. Tell me where it groups things that shouldn't be together, or splits things that should.
